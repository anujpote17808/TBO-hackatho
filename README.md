# TripMatch AI — Unified Travel Inventory Intelligence

**TBO Hackathon | 48-Hour Global Open Innovation Hackathon**

TripMatch AI combines a consumer-grade travel discovery marketplace (MakeMyTrip / Expedia / Booking.com style) with an enterprise B2B AI Inventory Intelligence OS that transforms fragmented multi-supplier travel inventory into clean, comparable, intelligently ranked, and bookable products.

> **Demo Data Notice:** All KPI figures, supplier feeds, pricing, guest ratings, and evaluation metrics in this prototype are **illustrative synthetic demonstration data** created for the TBO Hackathon, not TBO production statistics.

## Highlights

- **Premium Home & Floating Search Panel** — Immersive Taj Mahal sunrise parallax hero with dual `Experiences` & `Hotels` search tabs, 3D perspective **Explore Incredible India** destination cards, and interactive **My Trips** shortlist.
- **Destination Discovery** — Curated hubs for Agra, Jaipur, Goa, Delhi, Mumbai, and Kerala.
- **Sightseeing Marketplace & Offer Comparison** — Filterable experience cards with multi-supplier pricing and **Best Value vs Lowest Price** comparison modal preserving all inclusions, transfers, meals, and cancellation terms.
- **Hotel Search, Detail Gallery & Room Equivalence Matrix** — Booking.com-style search, multi-photo galleries, and attribute-level room comparison preventing false merges on name-similar rooms.
- **AI Inventory Intelligence OS** — Live client-side matching engine, configurable multi-factor Smart Ranking, sortable multi-supplier comparison matrix, content quality monitor, 9-layer architecture diagram, business impact KPIs, roadmap, and 9-slide Presentation Mode.
